About

I'm Mattia Giannattasio, a filmmaker, designer and photographer based in Parma, Italy.

I make short films and visual campaigns for technology brands and cultural institutions.

Explore my work →

The practice

Filmmaking, design and photography are different ways of exploring the same things: tools, visuals and stories shaping how we create and live.

Alongside commissioned work, I make independent films and photographic projects, and write The Creative Lens, a newsletter on creativity and design.
