Music Visuals.

Visual campaign · 2026

Music and the images around it. Visual direction for song releases.

For Noah Baine, I directed the visual side of the promotional content around two singles: Fallin’ and Destiny.

Each project starts with the music and develops the images around it.

Controlled light, darkness, grain and negative space became a common thread. Studio moments, backstage and performance pieces had to feel part of the same world across different formats. I documented the process in The Creative Lens.
