Destiny.

Visual campaign · 2026

Visual direction for Noah Baine. Recanati, small-town Italy and the influence of UK club culture.

Watch on Instagram ↗

We shot in Recanati, bringing everyday life in a small Italian town together with the UK club culture that shaped Noah Baine’s sound.

I directed the visual side of the single’s promotional content.

The images move between close portraits, people by the water and abrupt bursts of movement. The black-and-white treatment holds those different moments together.

Promotional visuals and moments from the Destiny project.
