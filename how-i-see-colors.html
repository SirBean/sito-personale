How I See Colors.

Photography · 2025

The Cinque Terre. A photo project about the colours I keep coming back to.

Two people can look at the same sky and describe different colours. I went to the Cinque Terre to photograph the ones I kept noticing.

Franco Fontana’s work had started that interest years earlier. This series was a way of paying attention to it again.

The series was later featured in Fisheye Magazine #76 in 2026.
